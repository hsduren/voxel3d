## English

**Voxel3D turns a single 2D image into a 3D model you can rotate in your browser and download.** Start with a photo, illustration, or concept render. Use it to prototype a game prop, explore a character design, or bring an object into a Blender or Unity project.

### From image to model

1. **Upload an image.** Drag a JPEG, PNG, or WebP into the upload area, or click to choose a file. Images can be up to 6 MB, with each side between 128 and 5,000 pixels.
2. **Review and generate.** Check the image preview, choose Standard textures or PBR materials, and generate the model. Standard uses 2 credits and PBR uses 3. New accounts receive 2 trial credits once. Additional credit packs are one-time purchases.
3. **Preview and download.** Rotate the result in the browser to inspect it from different angles, then choose an available export. Downloads require sign-in and are subject to a daily limit. Save your generated files within the 24-hour access window.

| Export | When to choose it |
| --- | --- |
| **GLB** | A single file that can bundle geometry, materials, and textures. A good starting point for Blender and glTF workflows. |
| **OBJ** | A widely supported mesh format for exchanging geometry. Materials and textures may come as separate files. |

For 3D printing, check scale and whether the mesh is watertight in your editing software before exporting STL for your slicer.

### Explore real examples

The **Featured** gallery includes a trail explorer, forest shrine, treasure chest, adventurer sword, potion shop, and stone guardian. Inspect the 3D previews for examples of characters, scenes, architecture, and props. **Mine** shows your private generations, and **Favorites** gives you a place to save examples you want to revisit.

For the clearest results, start with one well-lit subject against a simple background. A single image leaves the back and hidden surfaces for the generator to infer. Review those areas before putting a model into a production asset or print workflow.

## 简体中文

**Voxel3D 可以把一张二维图片转换成可在浏览器里旋转预览、下载的 3D 模型。** 你可以上传照片、插画或概念设计图，快速制作游戏道具原型、查看角色的立体效果，或把模型导入 Blender、Unity 继续处理。

### 从图片到 3D 模型

1. **上传图片。** 把 JPEG、PNG 或 WebP 拖进上传框，也可以点击选图。文件最大 6 MB，每边须在 128 至 5,000 像素之间。
2. **确认预览并生成。** 查看原图，选择标准纹理或 PBR 材质，再开始生成。标准模式消耗 2 积分，PBR 模式消耗 3 积分。新账号一次性获赠 2 积分，额外积分可按包单次购买。
3. **预览并下载。** 在网页中旋转模型，从不同角度检查效果，再选择可用的导出格式。下载需要登录，并有每日次数上限。生成结果可访问 24 小时，请在此期间保存所需文件。

| 导出格式 | 适用场景 |
| --- | --- |
| **GLB** | 可以把网格、材质和纹理打包进一个文件，适合 Blender 和 glTF 工作流。 |
| **OBJ** | 适合在不同建模软件之间交换网格，材质和贴图可能以独立文件提供。 |

如果要做 3D 打印，先在建模软件中检查模型尺寸和网格是否封闭，再导出 STL 给切片软件使用。

### 查看真实模型示例

**精选（Featured）** 展示了探险者、森林神龛、宝箱、冒险者长剑、药水商店和石像守卫，可直接查看角色、场景、建筑和道具的 3D 效果。**我的（Mine）** 展示你私有的生成结果，**收藏（Favorites）** 可以保存想回看的示例。

选图时，尽量使用光线清楚、背景简单、只有一个主要物体的图片。单张图里的背面和遮挡部分需要由模型推断，正式用于游戏制作或打印前，建议多转几个角度检查。
